Sales & Distribution Analytics

Sales MIS Dashboards

A management information system for vehicle sales & distribution — daily sales, target vs achievement, dealer performance, inventory, booking vs delivery, sales funnel and a consolidated management view, with variance, trend and exception analysis built in.
Every dashboard uses synthetic sample data for demonstration

MIS capabilities

  • Data consolidation — sales, finance, inventory and service data unified into one source of truth.
  • Data validation — duplicate, missing and inconsistent records identified (see Data Quality dashboard).
  • Excel automation — Power Query, Pivot, XLOOKUP/VLOOKUP and formula-driven automated reporting.
  • Ad-hoc analysis — answer MD/management questions directly from the underlying data.
  • Management presentation — key findings, charts and action points summarized for leadership.
